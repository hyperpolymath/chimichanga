=== Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for:

[arabic]
. *Code Review*: Reviewing and merging pull requests
. *Issue Triage*: Categorizing and responding to issues
. *Security*: Responding to security reports (see SECURITY.md)
. *Releases*: Managing version releases
. *Community*: Fostering a healthy contributor community
